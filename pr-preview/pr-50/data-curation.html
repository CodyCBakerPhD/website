Data Curation

Over the years, I have participated in the management, curation, and publication of dozens of high-impact datasets across many research institutions:

  • Directly, as the primary point person (Primary Engineer) or in some other engineering duty (Engineer, Fixer).
  • Indirectly, in a supervisory or advisory capacity.

    Group: Neural Dynamics and Computing   |   Location: Allen Institute for Brain Science   |   Modality: Microscopy   |   Role: Primary Engineer
    Dataset   |   Jupyter Notebook   |   Conversion Code

    Wrote custom code to standardize the mixed JSON/HDF5 metadata records for thousands of microscopy experiments and their associated visual stimuli.

    Published a total of 93.4 TB of visual cortex microscopy data on the associated NIH data archive.
